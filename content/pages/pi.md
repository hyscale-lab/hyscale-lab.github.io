Dmitrii Ustiugov is an Assistant Professor at [Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/). His work spans computer systems and architecture, with a focus on fast, scalable and resource-efficient cloud systems. Current projects target cloud and serverless architectures and system support for large-scale LLM inference serving. He leads the [vHive](https://vhive-serverless.github.io) open-source ecosystem for serverless experimentation, used in 30+ academic and 8+ industry organisations.

He received a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Edinburgh, co-advised by Profs. Boris Grot and Edouard Bugnion (EPFL), and was a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich with Prof. Ana Klimovic.
