This direction studies large-scale elastic generative-AI inference systems and how they can exploit heterogeneous hardware and software without sacrificing locality or responsiveness.

[ServerlessLLM](https://github.com/ServerlessLLM/ServerlessLLM) develops locality-enhanced serverless inference for large language models. The work appeared at OSDI 2024; see the [paper](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi24-fu.pdf) and the project repository {% cite fu2024serverlessllm %}.
