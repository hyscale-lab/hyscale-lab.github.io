This historical research direction explored how algorithms and the surrounding cloud stack could be designed together rather than optimized in isolation. The objective was faster, more scalable, and more power-efficient execution, from inference algorithms to workload management and systems support.

Examples include thought pruning for efficient self-consistency scaling and control-plane design that manages workloads rather than treating an AI cluster as a single undifferentiated resource {% cite hong2025slimsc lai2025workloads %}.
