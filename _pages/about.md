## HyScale Lab

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/hyscale-lab-logo.jpg" title="HyScale Systems and Cloud Architecture Lab" class="img-fluid rounded" %}

The **HyScale Systems and Cloud Architecture Lab** at [Nanyang Technological University Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) develops scalable and efficient systems across the cloud stack. Our research is organized around three pillars:

1. **Serverless cloud systems**
2. **Agentic cloud systems**
3. **Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems**

Explore our [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), or meet the lab on the [People]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}) page. Earlier work remains discoverable in the Historical research / Archive sections of Projects and Publications.

## PI / Supervisor

**Dmitrii Ustiugov** is an Assistant Professor at NTU Singapore. His work spans computer systems and architecture, with a focus on fast, scalable, and resource-efficient cloud systems and systems support for large-scale AI inference.

Dmitrii leads the [vHive](https://vhive-serverless.github.io/) open-source ecosystem for serverless experimentation and innovation across the distributed hardware/software stack. At the time of the source site, vHive was used by 30+ universities and supported or sponsored by 8 companies. He received a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Edinburgh, co-advised by [Boris Grot](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/bgrot/) and [Edouard Bugnion](https://people.epfl.ch/edouard.bugnion), and later conducted postdoctoral research at ETH Zurich with [Ana Klimovic](https://anakli.inf.ethz.ch/). In 2024, MIT Technology Review named him one of its [35 Innovators Under 35 in Asia-Pacific](http://tr35.mittrasia.com/awards), in the Visionary category, for open-source systems work.

The HyScale logo was designed by [Anna Kondrashova](https://www.behance.net/konvpalto00973).
