## HyScale Lab

The **HyScale Systems and Cloud Architecture Lab** at [Nanyang Technological University Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) builds scalable, efficient cloud systems and open-source research infrastructure. We study how systems software and computer architecture can make emerging cloud workloads faster, more elastic, and more resource-efficient.

## Research pillars

1. **Serverless cloud systems**
2. **Agentic cloud systems**
3. **Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems**

Our Serverless work develops systems for elastic computation and AI inference. Our Agentic work investigates supporting foundations for efficient reasoning and workload-aware AI infrastructure; the current projects do not claim to constitute a complete agentic cloud platform. Our Multi-modal and Embodied AI pillar is an active systems research direction, and we will add named artifacts only when they are ready to share.

## Open-source systems

### vHive

[vHive]({{ '/projects/cloud-serverless-vhive/' | relative_url }}) is our full-stack open-source environment for representative serverless experimentation across the distributed hardware/software stack. Researchers can explore the [vHive project website](https://vhive-serverless.github.io/), develop with the [vHive repository](https://github.com/vhive-serverless/vhive), and read the [ASPLOS 2021 paper]({{ '/assets/pdf/reap-asplos-2021.pdf' | relative_url }}) that accompanies its award-winning artifact.
