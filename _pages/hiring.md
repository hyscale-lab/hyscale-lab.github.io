The HyScale Lab welcomes enquiries from researchers interested in **Serverless cloud systems**, **Agentic cloud systems**, and **Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems**. Opportunities and funding availability change, so this page provides enquiry guidance rather than announcing vacancies.

## Prospective PhD students

Prospective PhD researchers may enquire about work spanning Serverless cloud systems, Agentic cloud systems, and Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems. A useful initial email includes a CV, academic transcripts (including BSc study), a motivation letter, and a TOEFL or IELTS certificate—or an explanation of the plan to obtain a missing language certificate.

Contact Professor Dmitrii Ustiugov before applying to a funding program:

- [Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/scholarships/npgs)
- [Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/for-graduate-studies/singapore-international-graduate-award-singa)
- [Alibaba–NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/alibaba-ntu-jri)
- [A\*STAR Graduate Scholarship (AGS)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/for-graduate-studies/a-star-graduate-scholarship-singapore)
- [AI Singapore PhD Fellowship](https://aisingapore.org/research/phd-fellowship-programme/)
- [A\*STAR Computing and Information Science Scholarship (ACIS)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/for-graduate-studies/a-star-cis-scholarship)
- [SIMTech–NTU](http://cs.simtechntu.ntu.edu.sg/)
- [Other NTU graduate scholarships](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/graduate-college/admissions)

## Postdoctoral researchers

Postdoctoral researchers may enquire about research connecting Serverless cloud systems, Agentic cloud systems, and Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems. Include a CV and a concise explanation of the proposed research connection. Prior guidance highlighted research publication at venues represented by [CSRankings](https://csrankings.org/) or open-source experience; it does not guarantee eligibility or availability.

Potential external funding resources include:

- [A\*STAR International Fellowship](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/for-post-doctoral-studies)
- [Lee Kuan Yew Postdoctoral Fellowship](<https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/lee-kuan-yew-postdoctoral-fellowship-(lkypdf)>)
- [NTU Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship](<https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/presidential-postdoctoral-fellowship-(ppf)>)
- [NTU Research Assistant Professorship](<https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/research-assistant-professorship-(rap)>)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions postdoctoral fellowships](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/worldwide/asean/news/get-ready-2023-msca-postdoctoral-fellowship-call)
- [Schmidt AI in Science](https://www.schmidtfutures.com/our-work/schmidt-ai-in-science-postdocs/)

## Interns and research assistants

Students and early-career researchers may enquire about internships or research-assistant work related to Serverless cloud systems, Agentic cloud systems, and Multi-modal and Embodied AI cloud systems. Send a CV and briefly describe relevant systems-programming, operating-systems, networking, computer-architecture, research, or open-source experience.

The [A\*STAR Research Attachment Programme (ARAP)](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/Scholarships/for-graduate-studies/a-star-research-attachment-programme) may be a useful external resource for eligible applicants; applicants should verify current terms directly with the program.
